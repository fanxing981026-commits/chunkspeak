Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026

ChunkSpeak is an English phrase learning website for Chinese learners. This policy explains what data is used when you visit the website or buy the Founding Pass.

Information We Process

  • Learning activity stored in your browser, such as saved phrases, mastered phrases, check-in progress, and custom practice text.
  • Basic technical data provided by the hosting platform, such as page requests and security logs.
  • Payment and order information processed by Creem when you buy the Founding Pass.

How We Use Information

Browser-stored learning data is used to make the learning interface work for you. Payment data is used to confirm purchases, deliver paid materials, prevent fraud, and provide customer support.

Payments

Payments are handled by Creem. ChunkSpeak does not directly collect or store full card numbers, payment credentials, or bank details. Creem may process information according to its own privacy and compliance policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be shared with service providers that are required to operate the website, process payments, prevent fraud, host content, or provide support.

Your Choices

You can clear browser-stored learning data by clearing site data in your browser. For purchase or account questions, contact support through the public support link below.

Contact

Support requests can be sent through the ChunkSpeak GitHub Issues page: https://github.com/fanxing981026-commits/chunkspeak/issues
